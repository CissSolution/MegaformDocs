# AI Form Designer on DNN

On DNN, MegaForm can be opened from the Persona Bar or from a MegaForm module placed on a page.

## Create a new form

1. Open **Persona Bar > Content > MegaForm**.
2. Select **New form**.
3. Choose **Create with AI**.
4. Describe the form you need.
5. Review the live preview and request any changes.
6. Save the result or open it in the full builder.

## Refine a form

Select **Edit** beside a form in the MegaForm panel, then open **AI Designer** from the builder
toolbar. Ask for field, layout, validation, workflow, or confirmation changes and inspect the form
canvas before saving.

## Put the form on a page

Use **Add to current page** from the Persona Bar panel, select the target pane, and confirm. See
[Managing MegaForm from the Persona Bar](dnn-persona-bar.md) for the complete administration flow.
